
Consumption:
- week_start: Beginning of the reporting week.
- asset_id: the unique id of a machine/s.
- Consumption (kWh): Total energy consumed by the machine/s.
- Consumption in Idle State (kWh): Energy consumed while the machine/s is not in operation.
- Consumption in Working State (kWh): Energy consumed while the machine/s is in operation.
- Power (kW): Power usage of the machine/s at a given time.
- Cost (€/kWh): Cost of energy consumption per kilowatt-hour for the machine/s.
