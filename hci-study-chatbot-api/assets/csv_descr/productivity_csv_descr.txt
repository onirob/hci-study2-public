
Productivity:
- week_start: Beginning of the reporting week.
- asset_id: the unique id of a machine.
- Actual Cicle Time (s): the actual cycle time in seconds. 
- Ideal Cicle Time (s): the ideal cycle time in seconds.
- Availability (%): Percentage of time the machine was available for production.
- Performance (%): Efficiency of the machine during production compared to its ideal cycle time.
- Quality (%): Percentage of good pieces produced compared to total pieces produced.
- Overall Equipment Efficiency (%): the overall equipment efficiency of the machine/s.
- Good pieces (#): how many good pieces have been produced.
- Bad pieces (#): how many bad pieces have been produced.
- Fully Productive Time (s): Total time machine spent operating at maximum capacity producing good pieces.
- Planned Production Time (s):  Total scheduled production time including downtime for maintenance and setup.
- Net Run Time (s): Actual operating time after accounting for planned and unplanned downtime.
- Run Time (s):  Total time machine was in operation, regardless of productivity or downtime.
